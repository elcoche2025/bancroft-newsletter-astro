Notes on the source schedule

Academic blocks
Three blocks per section, each holding exactly one small-group (NBSG) session. Block 1 runs 8:35–10:20 with its small group inside it. Blocks 2 and 3 are non-contiguous — their small groups sit in the midday window, so Block 2 is 10:25–11:25 plus 12:05–12:25, and Block 3 is 12:30–12:55 plus 2:20–3:10.
Block minutes
Block 1 carries 75 instructional minutes, Block 2 carries 80, and Block 3 carries 75 — 230 in total, identical in all three sections. The dashed hatching on the rail marks each block’s small-group segment; the dotted line bridges a block’s two halves.
Block 1 end time
Drawn to 10:20 rather than 10:15 so the band closes where ARC and Math NBSG actually end. At 10:15 the boundary would cut through the middle of a block in all three sections.
Transitions
All six transitions fall between blocks or between a block’s two segments. None interrupts instruction inside a segment.
Grid
The source workbook runs on a 5-minute grid from 8:00 AM to 3:15 PM. That grid is preserved here, so every block’s height is true to how long it actually runs.
Instructional minutes
All three sections total exactly 230 minutes of core instruction. Adding Strong Start (15 min) gives 245 minutes — the “4 hr 5 m” noted in the source file.
